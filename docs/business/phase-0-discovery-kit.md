# Phase 0 Discovery Kit

**Purpose:** Recruit and evaluate 3-5 design-partner programs without confusing interest, usage, and payment evidence.

## Interview Rules

- Interview coaches, mentors, and program coordinators separately from students when possible.
- Ask about the most recent real event before describing the proposed product.
- Prefer examples, artifacts, and observed behavior over opinions.
- Do not ask whether the person "likes" the idea.
- Do not count a compliment, waitlist signup, or free pilot as willingness to pay.
- Ask permission before recording. Written notes are sufficient.

## 20-Minute Interview Guide

### Context: 3 minutes

1. How many teams and active students are in your program?
2. How many competitions do you typically attend in a season?
3. Who is responsible for scouting and alliance-selection preparation?

### Last real event: 8 minutes

4. Walk me through scouting at your most recent event, from arrival to alliance selection.
5. What tools did you use: paper, spreadsheet, chat, an app, or memory?
6. How many people collected observations, and how were assignments coordinated?
7. When did information become incomplete, duplicated, outdated, or hard to find?
8. Tell me about the last alliance-selection or match-strategy decision you regretted. What information was missing?
9. Show me the sheet, document, app, or pick list if it is available.

### Consequence and existing spend: 4 minutes

10. What did the failure cost: time, missed observations, a poor pick, confusion, or no meaningful consequence?
11. Have you paid for scouting software, data, coaching tools, or related services before?
12. Who can approve a small seasonal software purchase, and which budget would it use?

### Pilot fit: 3 minutes

13. Which upcoming event could your program realistically use as a pilot?
14. Who would own setup, and how many scouts would actually use it?
15. What must work reliably for you to trust it during that event?

### Payment test: 2 minutes

16. If the pilot works at a real event, what would make continued access worth paying for?
17. Would USD $19-39 per program for the remainder of the season require approval? From whom?

Do not offer a discount in response to hesitation. Record the objection first.

## Phase 0 Recruitment Gate

Phase 1 implementation may begin when all conditions are true:

- At least 10 coach/program interviews are documented.
- At least three programs are `confirmed` for named upcoming events.
- Each confirmed program has a coach/mentor owner and at least two expected scouts.
- The repeated last-event problem matches the proposed shared scouting and pick-list workflow.
- Prediction limitations and API commercial-use risk are documented separately.
