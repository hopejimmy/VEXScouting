# VEXScouting Design Partner Pilot

**Status:** Outreach-ready draft
**Pilot price:** Free for one competition
**Intended recipient:** VEX V5 coach, mentor, or program coordinator

## The Offer

VEXScouting is building a private competition workspace that helps a program coordinate scouts, combine field observations with public event statistics, and prepare an alliance pick list.

We are inviting a small group of VEX programs to use the first version free for one real competition. This is a design-partner pilot, not a permanent free subscription.

## What Your Program Receives

- A private workspace for one named event.
- Access for one coach and the scouts participating in the pilot.
- Shared structured observations and private notes.
- A collaborative alliance pick list.
- Public VEX event and team statistics alongside your observations.
- Direct onboarding and event-day support from the developer.
- An export of your program's scouting data after the pilot.

The exact feature set may change as the pilot is prepared. We will confirm what is available before the event.

## What We Ask From You

- Name one coach or mentor as the pilot owner.
- Choose an actual upcoming competition for the pilot.
- Attend a 30-minute onboarding session before the event.
- Invite at least two scouts.
- Use the workspace during the event, targeting at least 20 observations and one completed pick list.
- Join a 20-minute feedback interview within 72 hours after the event.
- Allow us to measure feature usage and errors. We will not include private note contents in product analytics.

There is no obligation to buy after the pilot. At the end, we may offer continued founding access at an estimated USD $19-39 per program for the season. The offer and price will be shown before any payment decision.

## Privacy and Ownership

- Your program's scouting notes are private to invited members by default.
- We will not publish private observations or use them in public rankings without explicit permission.
- We will collect only the account and usage data needed to run and evaluate the pilot.
- The program can request an export or deletion of its pilot data.
- VEXScouting is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by VEX Robotics.
